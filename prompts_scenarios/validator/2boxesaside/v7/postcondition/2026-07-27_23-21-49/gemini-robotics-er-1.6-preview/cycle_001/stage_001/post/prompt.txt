You receive two distinct images in this exact order:
- the first attached image is I_pre, captured immediately before execution;
- the second attached image is I_post, captured immediately after execution.

I_post.

The provided structured scene context is the latest scene representation
generated at pipeline initialization or after the most recent replanning. It

and it must not override direct visual evidence from I_pre and I_post.

Do not assume that execution succeeded because an action was planned.
Validate the observed final state, not the intended action.

A postcondition that was already true in I_pre may be satisfied as a
final-state condition if it remains true in I_post. In that case, explicitly
state that it was already true and remained true. Do not claim that the action
caused a condition unless the visual comparison supports that change.

Use exactly one status for each condition:
- "satisfied": the comparison supports that the condition is true in I_post;
- "violated": the comparison supports that the condition is false in I_post;
- "uncertain": the evidence is insufficient, occluded, ambiguous, or
  inconsistent.

For every condition whose status is "violated" or "uncertain", also report a
progress_trend by directly comparing I_pre and I_post for that specific
condition, independent of whether it is now satisfied:
- "moved_closer": the scene visibly moved toward satisfying this condition
  between I_pre and I_post (e.g. an object is now nearer to, more aligned
  with, or less obstructed with respect to what the condition requires),
  even though it is not yet satisfied;
- "no_change": no visible change relevant to this condition, or the change
  is too small/ambiguous to call it a movement toward the condition;
- "moved_away": the scene visibly moved away from satisfying this condition.
Do not report "moved_closer" unless the visual comparison actually supports
it; when in doubt, use "no_change". Omit progress_trend entirely for
conditions whose status is "satisfied".
